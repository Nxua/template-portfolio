Our Story

Started small.
Stayed intentional.

Voss was founded in 2018 by two designers who walked away from a big agency to do better work for fewer clients. We've never had more than 10 people on the team — and that's by design.

Small means your project gets senior attention, every time. No account managers. No juniors doing the work. Just the people you hired.
